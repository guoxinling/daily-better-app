# Daily Better Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

Daily Better is designed to work without an account and without third-party tracking.

## Information We Collect
The app stores the following information locally on your device:
- Mood and check-in entries you log
- Reminder and appearance preferences

If you write text and tap Reflect, Daily Better sends the following information to the Daily Better reflection service to generate a one-time response:
- your current mood
- the current written note you entered for that check-in
- your locale
- a request identifier
- the app version
- an anonymous device token used for abuse protection and rate limiting

Daily Better does not require sign-in, and Timeline history is not uploaded as a running archive.

## How Information Is Used
Local device data is used to provide the app's features on your device, such as showing your Timeline and reminder settings.

Written reflection request data is used only to generate the requested reflection response and to protect the service from abuse. The backend is designed to avoid storing note text, reflection text, and suggested action text in operational metrics.

## Data Sharing
Daily Better does not sell your data and does not share your data with third parties for advertising.

Written reflection requests are processed through the Daily Better reflection service, which uses an AI model provider to generate the response for that request.

## Notifications
If you enable reminders, Daily Better uses Apple's local notification system to send reminder notifications on your device. Notification settings can be changed at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

## Data Retention
Your Timeline and settings data remain on your device unless you delete them, remove the app, or reset your device.

Anonymous backend metrics may retain request identifiers, token hashes, timestamps, app version, latency, provider model, token usage counts, success status, and error codes. They are designed not to retain note text, reflection text, or suggested action text.

## Children's Privacy
Daily Better is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes To This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.
