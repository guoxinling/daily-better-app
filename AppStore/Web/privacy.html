Daily Better

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 21, 2026

Daily Better is designed to work without an account and without third-party tracking.

Information We Collect

Daily Better does not collect personal information on our servers. The app stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Affirmations you save as favorites
  • Custom affirmations you create
  • Emoji mood entries you log
  • Reminder and appearance preferences

How Information Is Used

Your data is used only to provide the app's features on your device, such as saved affirmations, mood history, and reminder settings.

Data Sharing

Daily Better does not sell your data and does not share your data with third parties for advertising or analytics.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Daily Better uses Apple's local notification system to send reminder notifications on your device. Notification settings can be changed at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Data Retention

Your data remains on your device unless you delete it, remove the app, or reset your device.

Children's Privacy

Daily Better is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.
